How to be here

Walk, read, break, restore.

  • WASD walk. Shift sprint. Space jump.
  • Look captures the mouse. Esc gives it back. Hold the right mouse button to turn without capturing it. Arrow keys turn as well.
  • Click the ground to stroll. Click a plaque, or press E, to read.
  • 1–5 choose an instrument. Cast, F, or a locked click fires it. Hold for the ember beam.
  • R restores the sculpture in front of you. Shift+R restores the campus.
  • The bar above is the site map. It will carry you to any wing.

The powers are for the monuments, which were built to come apart. The marble study and the concrete cat are guests.
